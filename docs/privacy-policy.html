Privacy Policy — Reply Agent

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Reply Agent is a service operated by OptiGnosis Limited ("OptiGnosis", "we", "us", "our"). Reply Agent is an AI assistant that helps online sellers ("Business Customers") automatically respond to messages from their own customers ("End Customers") on WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to two groups of people:

  • Business Customers — the sellers who sign up for Reply Agent and connect their own messaging accounts.
  • End Customers — the people who message a Business Customer and whose conversations Reply Agent may respond to on that Business Customer's behalf.

2. Information We Collect

From Business Customers: account details (name, email, business name), the knowledge base you provide us (product catalog, prices, policies, FAQs, order data), and connection credentials for the messaging channels you link to your account via Meta's official connection flow.

From End Customers, on behalf of a Business Customer: your messaging handle or phone number, the content of messages you send to a Business Customer that uses Reply Agent, and any order information the Business Customer has shared with us to answer your questions accurately.

We do not knowingly collect information beyond what is necessary to operate the service described above.

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate and send automated replies to End Customer messages, grounded in the Business Customer's own knowledge base.
  • To detect when a message should be escalated to the Business Customer for a human decision, and to notify them.
  • To maintain conversation history so replies stay coherent across a conversation.
  • To improve reply quality for a specific Business Customer using their own correction history — never to train shared or public models, and never shared across unrelated Business Customers.
  • To provide the Business Customer with basic analytics about their own conversations.

4. AI-Generated Replies

Replies sent through Reply Agent are generated by artificial intelligence. End Customers are clearly informed within the conversation that they are interacting with an AI assistant, not a human, consistent with Meta's platform policies and our own commitment to transparency.

5. How We Share Information

We share information only as necessary to operate the service:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger are the channels through which messages are sent and received.
  • Anthropic — processes message content to generate AI replies, under Anthropic's own data-handling terms; message content is not used by Anthropic to train its models under our commercial terms.
  • Voyage AI — processes knowledge base content to power search/retrieval so replies are grounded in accurate information.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

6. Data Isolation Between Businesses

Each Business Customer's data — knowledge base, conversations, and customer information — is kept isolated from every other Business Customer's data. One seller's information is never used to answer another seller's customers.

7. Data Retention

We retain conversation and account data for as long as a Business Customer's account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping and legal compliance. A Business Customer may request deletion of their account data, including associated End Customer conversation history, by contacting us.

8. Security

We use per-tenant data isolation, encryption of personal information at rest, and access controls to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, including under Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date.
