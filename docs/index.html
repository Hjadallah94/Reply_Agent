How it works

  1. Tell it about your business.

    Your product catalog, prices, delivery areas, and policies — the agent only ever answers from what you've actually told it.

  2. It replies to the easy ones.

    Availability, prices, delivery windows, order confirmations — answered right away, any time of day.

  3. You handle the rest.

    Complaints, unusual requests, anything it isn't confident about — escalated to you with a suggested reply, never guessed.

Prefer to answer everyone yourself sometimes? You can switch the agent off for any or all of your channels whenever you want, and switch it back on later — nothing is deleted or disconnected.
